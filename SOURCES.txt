Adaptations: added separate packages; moved nested nodes and iterator to
separate files; replaced textbook main methods with test drivers; shortened
comments. Used the original hashTextbook formula as hash(), matching the
supplied section 3.4 screenshots: (key.hashCode() & 0x7fffffff) % m.
The current website's alternative bit-mixing hash was removed.
Added read-only capacity/bucketIndex/bucketKeys/keyAt methods for driver output.
Insertion, lookup, deletion, resizing and traversal logic are retained.
This is an adaptation of the companion source, not byte-for-byte printed text.
